What we do

Your one stop for a backyard oasis.

We can customize a deck of any shape or size to meet your needs, on the first, second or third floor, or a rooftop. We specialize in PVC and composite materials, and also build in pressure treated, cedar or exotic hardwoods.

01

Custom decks

From a compact backyard platform to a large multi-level deck, we design and build the structure so you get the function you need and the look you want.

02

Composite and PVC

Low-maintenance decking that stands up to Ontario weather, in the colours and profiles that suit your home.

03

Cedar, hardwood and pressure treated

Classic wood decks when you want the warmth of natural timber, including cedar and exotic hardwoods.

04

Pergolas and trellises

Shade and structure that complete the deck, built to match the rest of the project.

05

Fences, stone and more

Wood and composite fencing, interlock and stone work, wood and stone retaining walls, and concrete pads, so the whole yard comes together.

06

Outdoor living extras

Deck and landscape lighting, outdoor kitchens, cabanas and pool sheds, plus fire and water features, planned as part of the same build.

About us

Led by master carpenter Jeff Allain.

Jeff spent 12 years framing and customizing home builds, then found his calling in landscape construction. He has been designing and building outdoor spaces for homes in Oakville and the surrounding area for 20 years.

We have built over a thousand custom decks, and Jeff personally supervises all aspects of every job. He is a member of the North American Deck and Railing Association, and a licensed master carpenter. We also proudly offer the option of all-Canadian made materials.

  • Written guarantee on workmanship, and a 6-year warranty on our decks
  • Free quotes, with plans and permits as needed
  • Built efficiently, affordably, and to a high standard
